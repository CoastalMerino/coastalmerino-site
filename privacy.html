Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026

This site is run by Coastal Merino in Los Angeles, California. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you join our list, we collect the email address you enter. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.

How we use it

We use your email to send updates about Coastal Merino, such as launch news and first access to new products. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who handles it

Our email list is stored with Kit, the service we use to send email. The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs. Both only process information on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time, including under California law.

Contact

Email hello@coastalmerino.com with any question about your information.
