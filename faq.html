Won't wool be too hot for summer golf?

Superfine merino is lighter and more breathable than most people expect. It moves heat and moisture away from your skin, so it stays comfortable on a warm afternoon and doesn't turn cold and clammy when the weather shifts.

How does it fit?

A classic, tailored golf fit: clean through the body, with room to swing. Full measurements will be in our size guide at launch.

Where is it made?

How do I care for it?

Merino resists odor, so it needs washing less often than a synthetic polo. Full care instructions come with every polo.

The fabric

Is merino itchy?

Itch comes from coarse fibers. Superfine merino is far finer than the wool in a traditional sweater. Ours is finer than 18 micron, which is why it feels smooth against bare skin.

Will it keep me warm on a cold morning?

Merino insulates when it's cool and breathes when it warms up, so it works from an early tee time through the afternoon. On colder days, wear it under a sweater or quarter zip.

Why no synthetics at all?

Most performance polos are polyester and elastane. Merino already breathes, wicks moisture, resists odor, and holds its shape, so we don't blend in anything else.
