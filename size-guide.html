How to measure

The easiest way: lay your best-fitting polo flat and measure it, then compare to the chart.

Chest

Measure straight across, one inch below the armholes, from side seam to side seam.

Body length

From the highest point of the shoulder, where it meets the collar, straight down to the hem.

Shoulder

Straight across the back, from one shoulder seam to the other.

Sleeve

From the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve opening.

Between sizes?

Email hello@coastalmerino.com with your height, weight, and the size you usually wear in golf polos, and we'll recommend one.
