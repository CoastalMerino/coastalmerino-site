Why merino

The fabric does the work, so you don't think about it.

Most golf polos are polyester and elastane. Ours is wool and nothing else. Merino moves heat and moisture away from your skin, resists odor, and springs back into shape, which is everything a performance polo is supposed to do.

More on the fabric

Our standards

What goes into every polo, and what never does.

100% merino

Every fiber in the fabric is merino wool. No blends.

Nothing synthetic

No polyester, nylon, or elastane anywhere in the fabric.

No glued collar

Sewn with a woven interlining instead of fused with adhesive, so it holds its shape wash after wash.
