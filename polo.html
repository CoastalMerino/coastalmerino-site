The details

How it's put together.

No fused parts, no stretch fibers, and nothing in it that isn't there for a reason. Confirm trims with factory

  1. Self-fabric collar

    Sewn with a woven interlining instead of glued, so it keeps its shape for the life of the polo.

  2. Corozo buttons

    Cut from the tagua nut, a natural alternative to plastic buttons.

  3. Cotton thread

    Mercerized cotton thread throughout, so even the seams are natural fiber.

  4. Collar stays

    Removable stays keep the collar points sharp, on the course and at dinner.

Questions

Won't wool be too hot for summer golf?

Superfine merino is lighter and more breathable than most people expect. It moves heat and moisture away from your skin, so it stays comfortable on a warm afternoon and doesn't turn cold and clammy when the weather shifts.

How does it fit?

A classic, tailored golf fit: clean through the body, with room to swing. Model measurements and the full chart will be in our size guide at launch.

How do I care for it?

Merino resists odor, so it needs washing less often than a synthetic polo. Full care instructions come with every polo.
