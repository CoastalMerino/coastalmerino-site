Our story

A golf polo with nothing synthetic in it.

I couldn't find a golf polo without polyester in it. Everything on the rack was synthetic, and the natural options weren't built for a real round. So we made one.

I grew up in Northern California, played baseball at USC, and keep synthetics out of my life wherever I can. Merino breathes, resists odor, and moves with you. It should be the standard fabric in golf, and this polo is where we start.

Jack Basseer, co-founder

Our standards

Four rules for every polo.

We'd rather make one polo the right way than a full line that cuts corners.

  1. 100% merino

    Every fiber in the fabric is merino wool. No blends.

  2. Nothing synthetic

    No polyester, nylon, or elastane anywhere in the fabric.

  3. No glued collar

    Sewn with a woven interlining instead of fused with adhesive, so it holds its shape wash after wash.

  4. Full sourcing

    Before launch we'll publish where the wool is grown, where it's knit, and where every polo is sewn.
