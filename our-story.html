Our story

A golf polo with nothing synthetic in it.

Why we made it

We couldn't find the polo we wanted, so we're making it.

I grew up in Northern California playing golf and baseball, and went on to play baseball at USC. Nearly everything I trained and competed in was synthetic. Activewear runs on polyester, nylon, and elastane, and the natural fiber options never kept up: cotton soaks through by the back nine, and most wool isn't built for a full swing.

So we made a golf polo from 100% merino that performs the way it should. It breathes, resists odor, and moves with you. Merino should be the standard fabric in golf, and this polo is where we start.

Jack Basseer, co-founder

How we're building it

Three decisions behind every polo.

The fiber

Superfine New Zealand merino, finer than 18 micron, knit into a polo with no polyester, nylon, or elastane in the fabric.

The make

A self-fabric collar sewn with a woven interlining instead of glued, so it keeps its shape for the life of the polo.

In the open

Before launch we'll publish where the wool is grown, where it's knit, and where every polo is sewn.

Our standards

What goes into every polo, and what never does.

100% merino

Every fiber in the fabric is merino wool. No blends.

Nothing synthetic

No polyester, nylon, or elastane anywhere in the fabric.

No glued collar

Sewn with a woven interlining instead of fused with adhesive, so it holds its shape wash after wash.

Full sourcing
