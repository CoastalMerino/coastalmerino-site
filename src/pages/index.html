Why we made it

I grew up in Northern California playing golf and baseball, and went on to play baseball at USC. Nearly everything I trained and competed in was synthetic. Activewear runs on polyester, nylon, and elastane, and the natural fiber options never kept up: cotton soaks through by the back nine, and most wool isn't built for a full swing.

So we made a golf polo from 100% merino that performs the way it should. It breathes, resists odor, and moves with you. Merino should be the standard fabric in golf, and this polo is where we start.

Jack Basseer, co-founder
