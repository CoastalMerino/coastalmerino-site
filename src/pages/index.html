Why merino

Merino vs. polyester.

Most golf polos are polyester and elastane. Here's what changes when the whole shirt is wool.

PropertyMerinoPolyester
Where it comes fromGrown by sheep, renewed every yearA plastic, made from petroleum
OdorNaturally resists odorTends to hold onto odor after a sweaty round
TemperatureBreathes when it's warm, insulates when it coolsBuilt to wick sweat, not to regulate temperature
FeelSoft, matte, natural drapeSlick synthetic finish
