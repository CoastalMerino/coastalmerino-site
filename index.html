The fabric

Superfine merino, built for eighteen holes and what comes after.

Knit from New Zealand merino finer than 18 micron, in a classic piqué.

Feel

Smooth, soft, and light on the skin.

Temperature

Cool on a warm afternoon round, comfortable when the marine layer rolls in.

Odor resistance

Still fresh when you sit down for dinner.

Wrinkle resistance

Holds its shape in a golf bag, a carry-on, or a long day.

Our standards

What goes into every polo, and what never does.

100% merino

Every fiber in the fabric is merino wool. No blends.

Nothing synthetic

No polyester, nylon, or elastane anywhere in the fabric.

No glued collar

Sewn with a woven interlining instead of fused with adhesive, so it holds its shape wash after wash.

Full sourcing

Before launch we'll publish where the wool is grown, where it's knit, and where every polo is sewn.

Why we made it

I grew up in Northern California playing golf and baseball, and went on to play baseball at USC. Nearly everything I trained and competed in was synthetic. Activewear runs on polyester, nylon, and elastane, and the natural fiber options never kept up: cotton soaks through by the back nine, and most wool isn't built for a full swing.

So we made a golf polo from 100% merino that performs the way it should. It breathes, resists odor, and moves with you. Merino should be the standard fabric in golf, and this polo is where we start.

Jack Basseer, co-founder

Questions

Before you tee off

When does the first polo launch?

We're finalizing our first production run now. Join the list and you'll hear first, before anything goes on sale.

Won't wool be too hot for summer golf?

Superfine merino is lighter and more breathable than most people expect. It moves heat and moisture away from your skin, so it stays comfortable on a warm afternoon and doesn't turn cold and clammy when the weather shifts.

How does it fit?

A classic, tailored golf fit: clean through the body, with room to swing. Full measurements will be in our size guide at launch.

Where is it made?

Our wool is New Zealand merino. We're finalizing our mill and sewing partners now, and before launch we'll share exactly where every polo is made, from the wool to the final stitch.

How do I care for it?

Merino resists odor, so it needs washing less often than a synthetic polo. Full care instructions come with every polo.

Can my club's pro shop carry Coastal Merino?

We'd like to hear from you. Golf professionals and buyers can reach us at wholesale@coastalmerino.com.
