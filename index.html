Odor resistance

Wrinkle resistance

Merino fibers spring back. It holds its shape in a golf bag, a carry-on, or a long day.

Our standards

100% merino

Every fiber in the fabric is merino wool. No blends.

Nothing synthetic

No polyester, nylon, or elastane anywhere in the fabric.

No glued collar

The collar is sewn with a woven interlining instead of fused with adhesive, so it holds its shape wash after wash.

Why we made it

I couldn't find a golf polo without polyester in it. Everything on the rack was synthetic, and the natural options weren't built for a real round. So we made one.

I grew up in Northern California, played baseball at USC, and keep synthetics out of my life wherever I can. Merino breathes, resists odor, and moves with you. It should be the standard fabric in golf, and this polo is where we start.

Jack Basseer, co-founder

Won't wool be too hot for summer golf?

Superfine merino is lighter and more breathable than most people expect. It moves heat and moisture away from your skin, so it stays comfortable on a warm afternoon and doesn't turn cold and clammy when the weather shifts.

How does it fit?

A classic, tailored golf fit: clean through the body, with room to swing. Full size details come with launch.

Where is it made?

Our wool is New Zealand merino. We're finalizing our mill and sewing partners now, and before launch we'll share exactly where every polo is made, from the wool to the final stitch.

How do I care for it?

Merino resists odor, so it needs washing less often than a synthetic polo. Full care instructions come with every polo.
